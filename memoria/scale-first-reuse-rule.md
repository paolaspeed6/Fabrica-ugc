the owner (2026-10-13): "sempre valorizar a escala". Generated scenes are the expensive part, so reuse them across platforms, but every version must look original: its own hook, its own CTA, its own edit template and font. A version that only reorders scenes is NOT acceptable (the owner rejected banqueta_tiktok_reels_v1 for "só mudou a ordem").

**Why:** more distinct, professional versions per generation = more creatives to test at the same R$ cost per scene, without platforms seeing duplicates.

**How to apply:** for every product, render one version per platform with montar_video_pro.py "estilo": shopee (v2 "vitrine": frosted-glass card, callouts, spec chips; APPROVED by the owner; old Inter Display look is now "classico"), tiktok (v3: Bebas Neue condensed uppercase, cascading lines, minimal white CTA; APPROVED by the owner; the white caption-box version was rejected as amateur), reels (Instagram/Facebook; DM Serif editorial bottom-left, uppercase label, outlined CTA, slow fades; APPROVED by the owner, reuse it). Fonts (OFL) in video-teste/fontes. Claude writes a NEW hook and CTA for each version, rotating angles (POV, desire, pain, curiosity, measure/proof, comparison, offer) and never repeating one across versions of the same product. Keep claims to what the listing supports. When proposing anything, prefer the option that multiplies output per R$ and per minute of the owner's time.

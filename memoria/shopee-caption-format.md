the owner's rule from 2026-10-15: Shopee captions are one plain description plus hashtags, written to a professional standard. He believes this converts best.

Shopee's own blog backs it up. It asks for "uma legenda atrativa" and "hashtags relevantes para ajudar o algoritmo", with no redirects to other platforms. No source confirmed a caption character limit, so keep captions under 150 characters to be safe.

Format:
- Line 1: product name + model/variant + the 1-2 strongest proven facts (unit count, size, rating, material). No emojis, no "link no comentário".
- Line 2: 4 hashtags: product, category, use or audience, then #achadinhosshopee.

Example: "Tapete higiênico Espaço de Bicho 60 x 50 cm, 200 unidades. Mais grosso, com atrativo canino.\n#tapetehigienico #pet #cachorro #achadinhosshopee"

**How to apply:** put this in p*.json "legendas.shopee" for every product. Reels and Facebook captions keep the hook, the CTA and the pinned-comment link ([[distribution-plan]], [[videos-prontos-page]]).

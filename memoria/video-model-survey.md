the owner asked (2026-10-14) which models match the SOYO video quality, for best cost-benefit. Survey done at US$0 (WaveSpeed /api/v3/models: 198 image-to-video models; Arena human-vote rankings; WaveSpeed blog pricing tables). Conclusion sent to the owner: keep **Seedance 1.5 Pro Fast** ([[reference-videos-quality-bar]]).

Budget math: R$3 cap = ~US$0.53 per video; minus 3 Seedream edits (US$0.081) leaves ~US$0.15 max per 5 s 720p clip, with no redo margin.

720p, 5 s clip (Arena image-to-video Elo, Sep 2026 / price):
- Seedance 1.5 Pro (Fast): 1307 / US$0.10 with no audio (measured; WaveSpeed blog: 720p US$0.10 without audio, US$0.20 with audio, 1080p US$0.15/0.30)
- Kling 2.5 Turbo: 1276 / US$0.21
- Hailuo 2.3 Fast 768p: 1262 / US$0.19 per 6 s clip
- Vidu Q2 Pro Fast: 1223 / US$0.08 (720p: $0.04 1 s, $0.08 5 s, $0.11 8 s); cheapest but clearly lower quality
- PixVerse V6 ~US$0.25-0.30; Gemini Omni Flash ~US$0.10/s; Grok Imagine 1.5 ~US$0.14/s at 720p; MiniMax H3 ~US$0.10/s at 768p; Seedance 2.0 Fast ~US$0.12/s; Kling v3 turbo std US$0.112/s (measured). These top-ranked models cost ~R$9-12 per video, only for a single hero product.

The catalog base_price unit varies (per clip, per second, or the minimum config), so only Seedance 1.5 and Kling v3 prices are measured. Re-run the survey from time to time: new models and price drops come fast.
